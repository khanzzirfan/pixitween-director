Extends the A2 converter so the director's 3D layers play on the engine's three layer: model3d product shots (the label can), and the cartoon world (stage3d with its actor3d and prop3d members: camera, lights, ground, backdrop, glTF actors with a clip and blocking marks, props). Toon shading, curved walls and bone attach become warnings for later work.

## Manual gates (owner; Ralph never runs these)

- MG-1: owner opens http://localhost:5173/aep-motion.html?film=<id> for one product-3D film and one cartoon film and judges the 3D scene.

### US-001: model3d → three layer (primitive can with label, glTF, view rotation, camera fit)

As a viewer, I want the 3D product shot to turn on the new engine so that product films keep their hero moment.

**Steps**

1. Read engine/docs/10-aep-adapter.md 'model3d' and shared/aep/layer.ts (aepModel3D* schemas: viewport, source, textures, material, view, lighting, shadow, camera).
2. Create web/src/motion/aep/model3d.ts exporting convertModel3D(layer, ctx) returning a three layer: size from viewport, transparent background, base transform from A2's baseLayer, lighting preset, shadow opacity, one object: a cylinder for source.shape 'can' (radius 0.5, height proportions.height) with material.map = textures.label and color = textures.side, or a gltf object for a gltf source; view.rotation (keyframed [x,y,z] degrees) → object rotation.x/y/z tweens; camera.fov kept and camera.position.z chosen so the object height × camera.fit fills the viewport height (z = h*fit / (2*tan(fov/2))).
3. Wire it into convertLayers in convert.ts in place of the A2 warning.
4. Create web/src/motion/aep/model3d.test.ts with the stored 'hero:product3d' layer shape.

**Acceptance criteria**

- [ ] The stored product3d layer converts to a three layer 441x1296 with one cylinder object of height 3.075 whose material.map is the label asset
- [ ] Its view.rotation keyframes [4,-18,0] -> [4,18,0] over 120 frames give a tween on object:<id> 'rotation.y' from -18 to 18 over 4 s
- [ ] camera.position.z equals 3.075 * 1.5 / (2 * tan(15°)) within 1e-6 for fov 30, fit 1.5
- [ ] The converted doc passes parseMotionDoc and validateTargets
- [ ] `npm run typecheck` passes
- [ ] `npm test` passes (offline, no network)
- [ ] `npm run lint` passes
