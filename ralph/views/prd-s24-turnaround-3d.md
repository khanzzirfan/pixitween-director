The 3D product stops showing its front on its back and stops guessing its depth. The real cut-out is placed in a three-panel sheet and Muse's image model draws the right side and the back beside it; code splits and measures the views (front kept, back the same outline, colours close, depth from the side), Muse checks them against the photo with one redraw at most; the body's front half wears the real photo, its back half the drawn (or a supplied) back, at the measured depth. The engine gets a back material for the body. Off by default (TURNAROUND=1); any failure keeps today's body. Leaves out: boxes, an image-to-3D mesh, characters, an approval gate.
