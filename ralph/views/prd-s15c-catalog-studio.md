The browser side of Catalog Crew: import a feed on a finished film, watch the catalog wall fill with one live player and one hidden capture player, edit a price and see it update, open a product page mock, and optionally publish MP4s through Clippy's renderer into a feed CSV. Everything except Publish plays live in the browser.

- MG-1: owner confirms the Clippy render tool's name, arguments and result shape (composition JSON in, MP4 URL out), whether it renders model3d layers and the MSDF fonts, and sets CLIPPY_RENDER_TOOL.

- MG-3: owner checks the catalog page in Chrome with 30 products: no lost WebGL context in the console after selecting 10 products in a row.
