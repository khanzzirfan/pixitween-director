# Engine needs

`reacthub-pixitweenjs` is a pre-existing dependency and is never edited here. When a story finds a gap, add a row.
The owner decides whether to patch the engine, publish `0.3.0-alpha.N` and bump the dependency.

| Date | Story | Need | Where in the engine | Proposed API | Workaround in this repo | Status |
|------|-------|------|---------------------|--------------|-------------------------|--------|
| 2026-10-28 | S3a US-022 | **Multi-scene films throw when time changes.** A root composition with two or more `precomp` layers where any scene has an `image` layer renders its first frame, then throws `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'alphaMode')` (at `getAdjustedBlendModeBlend`) on the first seek or play tick, and the canvas goes blank. Plus 94 Pixi warnings `A TextureSource managed by Assets was destroyed instead of unloaded`. One scene with the same layers is fine (seek, play and camera keyframes all work); two scenes with no images are fine; an image in scene 1 only or scene 2 only both fail; a distinct `src` per scene does not help. Seen in headless Chrome at `?fixture=1&name=s03a/two-scene-dolly` (engine 0.3.0-alpha.3). | `PixiPreComp` / `AepPrecompRenderer.tsx` (the precomp RenderTexture) and the sprite texture lifecycle; the texture appears to be destroyed while still displayed | Do not destroy Assets-managed TextureSources on unmount (use `Assets.unload`), and keep a precomp's RenderTexture alive while its sprite is mounted | None found. Single-scene films play. Until fixed, a multi-scene film cannot be scrubbed or played in the browser (blocks the S5b capture, S8a export and every demo) | open |
| 2026-10-29 | S7b US-014 | **A `video` layer inside a scene precomp blanks the whole film.** A two-scene film (scene 1: shape and text; scene 2: bg, product, headline and a video clip layer added by `swapInClip`) shows scene 1, but after seeking to scene 2 + 0.5 s the canvas is empty: no background, no headline and no clip frame, so the clip's burned-in timestamp could not be read. The console shows `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'paused')` at `VideoSource.destroy -> _configureAutoUpdate -> _isSourcePlaying`, thrown while React unmounts the video layer, and a log `video sprite reverse complete s2:clip`. The same film with scene 2 left as plain motion (no clip) draws correctly at the same time, and setting the layer to `muted: true` does not change it. Seen in Chrome at `/clip-check.html` (add `?noclip=1` or `?muted=1` to compare), engine 0.3.0-alpha.3. Probably the same texture-lifecycle fault as the multi-scene row above. | The video sprite teardown in `reacthub-pixitweenjs` (`VideoSource.destroy` runs after the `<video>` element is already null) and the precomp RenderTexture lifecycle | Null-check the video element before reading `paused` in destroy, and do not destroy a TextureSource that Assets manages | None. Generated clips are placed correctly in the composition (server tests pass) but cannot be played back in the browser until this is fixed, which blocks the S7b demo, export of generated scenes and the MG-3 alignment sign-off | open |
