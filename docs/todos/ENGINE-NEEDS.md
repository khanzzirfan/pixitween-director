# Engine needs

`reacthub-pixitweenjs` is a pre-existing dependency and is never edited here. When a story finds a gap, add a row.
The owner decides whether to patch the engine, publish `0.3.0-alpha.N` and bump the dependency.

| 2026-10-28 | S3a US-022 | **Multi-scene films throw when time changes.** A root composition with two or more `precomp` layers where any scene has an `image` layer renders its first frame, then throws `TypeError: Cannot read properties of null (reading 'alphaMode')` (at `getAdjustedBlendModeBlend`) on the first seek or play tick, and the canvas goes blank. Plus 94 Pixi warnings `A TextureSource managed by Assets was destroyed instead of unloaded`. One scene with the same layers is fine (seek, play and camera keyframes all work); two scenes with no images are fine; an image in scene 1 only or scene 2 only both fail; a distinct `src` per scene does not help. Seen in headless Chrome at `?fixture=1&name=s03a/two-scene-dolly` (engine 0.3.0-alpha.3). | `PixiPreComp` / `AepPrecompRenderer.tsx` (the precomp RenderTexture) and the sprite texture lifecycle; the texture appears to be destroyed while still displayed | Do not destroy Assets-managed TextureSources on unmount (use `Assets.unload`), and keep a precomp's RenderTexture alive while its sprite is mounted | None found. Single-scene films play. Until fixed, a multi-scene film cannot be scrubbed or played in the browser (blocks the S5b capture, S8a export and every demo) Re-checked 2026-10-29 on a plain two-scene film (no clip) at `/clip-check.html?noclip=1`: seeking into scene 2 draws it, but playing through the scene boundary throws the same `alphaMode` error and blanks the canvas, so the bug is still there in 0.3.0-alpha.3 (no newer version is published). Tried and failed: keeping every scene precomp mounted for the whole film and moving its start with `startTime` (scene 2 then draws at time 0 and the crash still happens at the boundary). The engine's precomp renderer pools its RenderTextures by size (`p$`, `m$`, `g$` and the `AepPrecompRenderer` component near line 48080 of `dist/index.es.js`); the null texture source seems to come from there or from the `Assets` cache. | **closed 2026-10-30** (confirmed on alpha.5 in headless Chrome: played through the scene 1 → 2 boundary at `/clip-check.html?noclip=1` with no `alphaMode` error and no destroyed-Assets warning; `/clip-check.html` at scene 2 + 0.5 s shows the headline and clip frame with no `paused` error). Earlier status: **Root cause found 2026-10-29:** the image sprite's `dispose` calls `texture.destroy(true)` on a texture `Assets` still caches whenever the URL is not http(s), so the next load returns a dead texture. Patch in `reacthub-pixitweenjs/docs/director-uplift/01-texture-teardown-fixes.md` (Fix 1). **Update 30 Oct: fixed in `0.3.0-alpha.4`, current `0.3.0-alpha.5`** (the image sprite now releases textures through a per-URL reference count and `Assets.unload`).
