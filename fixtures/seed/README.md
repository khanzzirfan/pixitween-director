Notes for test authors
- A SAM mask raster covers the object's **box** (`bounds`), not the whole frame; paint it into a full-frame alpha at `(left, top)`.
- Muse Image returns 1920x1280 when asked for 1536x1024; real sizes come from the PNG header.
- The images are generated by Muse Image 1.0 or our own pipeline and are used here as demo fixtures; list them in `ASSET-CREDITS.md`.
